Royal Cric Clash – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Royal Cric Clash ("the app") is a cricket stat-card game for 2 to 8 players, played against bots, with friends online, or with friends on the same Wi-Fi network or nearby over Bluetooth. This policy explains what information the app uses. It is provided by Royal Clash Creation ("we", "us").

Nearby play permissions

For the optional "Nearby" mode the app asks for Bluetooth, nearby devices and location permission. Android requires location permission to find nearby phones. The app does not read, store or send your location; the permission is used only to connect phones to each other.

Information we do not collect

The app has no sign-up. We do not ask for or collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

Information the app uses

  • Anonymous ID. To let you play online, the app signs you in anonymously with Firebase Authentication. This creates a random ID that is not linked to your identity.
  • Game room data. When you create or join an online room, the app stores the room code, the player name you choose, which players are in the room, and the stats picked in each round, in Google Cloud Firestore. Rooms are deleted automatically shortly after a game ends or after a short time if nobody joins. A room password, if you set one, is stored with the room until it is deleted.
  • Same Wi-Fi mode. In this mode, game data is exchanged directly between the phones on your local network and does not go through our servers. The app may use your device's local network address to let nearby players find your game.
  • Data on your device. Settings such as your coins, mute choice and game progress are stored on your phone only.
  • Player statistics. Cricket statistics shown on the cards come from public Cricsheet data (cricsheet.org). The app may download an updated statistics file from the internet; this request does not include personal information.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. This may include personalized ads, depending on your device settings. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). For more information, see Google's policy at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob – Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) – Firebase privacy information

Sharing and retention

We do not sell your information. Online room data is deleted automatically as described above. The anonymous ID may remain in Firebase Authentication until it is removed; you can ask us to delete it using the contact below. Advertising data is handled by Google under its own policies.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and deletion requests

You can uninstall the app at any time, which removes all data stored on your device. To request deletion of any data linked to your anonymous ID, contact us at the email below and include the room code if you have one.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed.
